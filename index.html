Key Takeaways


    What is the form of media?
    Media form is the type of media it is, such as a poster, a magazine cover, a newspaper front page or an advertisement.

    What are conventions?
    We know that conventions are the features that audiences expect to find in a particular form, such as a slogan, a central figure and a call to action on an advertisement.

    Why do conventions matter?
    Conventions let audiences recognise a media text in seconds, read it in the right order, and know what they are being asked to do.

    What is a call to action?
    We know that a call to action is a direct instruction that tells the audience what to do next, such as where to go or what to buy.

    What is a target audience?
    We know that the target audience is the particular group of people a media text is designed to reach.

    How should we identify a target audience?
    We should never just name an audience. We must always prove it with evidence from the words, images and design of the text.

    What is mode of address?
    Mode of address is the way a media text speaks to its audience, through direct address, tone, register and choice of words.

    What is gaze?
    Gaze is the direction in which a person in an image is looking, and the relationship this creates with the viewer.

    What is representation?
    Representation is the way people, places or ideas are shown in a media text, and the impression this creates for the audience.

    Why do placement and context matter?
    We know that where and when a media text appears tells us a great deal about its audience, because each place is visited, read or passed by particular people.


Words that I Learnt from the Story 📚

Surmise
Meaning: To guess or infer something based on limited evidence or clues.
Example: From the scattered notes and unfinished calculations, the detective surmised that the scientist had been interrupted.

Cognise
Meaning: To know, perceive, or become aware of something; to understand mentally.
Example: It took the committee several months to cognise the full implications of the proposed reforms.

Expound
Meaning: To explain or present something in detail and systematically.
Example: The historian expounded the causes of the revolution with remarkable clarity and precision.
